## Safe interpretation

Large leave-one-out beta delta means the grouped beta target is sensitive to a single rollout. If an extreme rollout also has large metric z-scores, treat that condition as unstable/noisy and collect more repeats or use a robust aggregation rule before training a deployable Objective Selector.
